      Payments

      Students pay with MTN or Airtel mobile money through MarzPay and get a pass for a time. Notes you mark "Free" stay open to everyone. While this is off nothing changes: every note is open.

      NamePrice (UGX)HoursOn
      The price is set here and nowhere else: the app cannot change what a pass costs. 24 hours = 1 day, 168 = 1 week, 720 = 30 days, 2160 = 90 days.
