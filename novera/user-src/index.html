How cash-outs work

1

Choose Mobile Money or Bank Transfer and enter your payout details.

2

Enter the amount you want and tap Request Cash Out.

3

Enter your 4-digit Payout PIN to confirm. Never set one before? You'll be asked to create it right there — choose it yourself, remember it, and never share it with anyone (not even "customer service").

4

Your request is processed automatically and the net amount (after the service fee) is sent straight to your mobile money number or bank account.

5

Track the status any time under Account → Ledger → Cash Outs.

About Us
A Novera Feature
Own a Piece of the Next Supernova

Every plan on Novera represents a stake in a real, tracked cycle of growth — the same principle that has always sat behind serious investing, wrapped in a platform built for how people actually save and grow money today.

We started Novera with one belief: that access to real returns should not depend on how much capital you start with. Whether you are securing your first Comet plan or holding a Singularity-tier position, every member is on the same ladder, governed by the same transparent cycle and the same math — no hidden tiers, no special deals.

Behind every plan is a simple mechanism: your entry funds a fixed-length cycle, and at maturity your full return lands in your wallet, ready to withdraw or reinvest. No guesswork, no partial payouts along the way — you always know exactly what you are owed and when.

Novera exists for the people who want their money working as hard as they do — whether that is a boda rider growing a first Comet plan into a deposit on a better bike, or a trader compounding steadily into the higher tiers. This is your platform to build with, one cycle at a time.
