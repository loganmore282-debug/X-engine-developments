## Look in one line
Warm **chocolate brown on cream**: page cream `#f9f3ea`-ish, white cards, brown gradient buttons (about `#6a4220` -> `#4a2c15`, right end slightly lighter),
dark brown text `#3b1f0f`, mid brown `#7a5c3e`, gold accents (`#d9a21b` ring, gold bars, gold figures `#8a5a14`), green only for DAILY figures (`#2e7d32`).
Font: Roboto, headings with wide letter-spacing and bold. Region **Nigeria**: `+234`, currency **₦** with two decimals (`₦3,600.00`).
Outline icons in brown (lock, shield, eye, gift, envelope, home, receipt, megaphone, people, person). Everything rounded (fields about 28 px radius, buttons about 24 px).
